# Product direction (decided 2026-10-27, after review v005)

**Reroll turns a batch of your Medal clips into one postable video, automatically.**

Why: a single-clip Reroll edit (v005) is close to what Medal's own editor already does (trim, 9:16
crop, captions, post to TikTok/Shorts). Medal does not make multi-clip montages; its own page points
to a desktop editor for that. v003 (four clips, story kept, best ace last) is the edit Edward would
post and the one that takes real work by hand.

- Default: collect new clips; offer "make a video from your recent plays".
- Single-clip edits stay as a minor feature.
- Export: game audio only; the player adds a sound in TikTok. The generated Reroll beat is optional.
- Approved look: whole game frame + blurred fill, game colours, no effects, watermark.
