From moment to post in four steps

  1. Turn on the replay buffer

    Recording stays off until you switch it on. While it’s on, only the last 15, 30, 45 or 60 seconds are kept.

  2. Say “Clip that”

    Or press the hotkey, even over a full-screen game. The moment is saved to your current session.

  3. Collect a session

    Clips pile up as you play. You can also bring in exports from OBS or any recorder that saves to a folder.

  4. Export a vertical video

    Trim, reorder and pick a layout, then export a 9:16 MP4 ready to upload to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

Two ways to use Clip That

Clip That for Mac

In beta testing

Hands-free capture while you play. With the replay buffer on, saying “Clip that” saves the previous 30 seconds plus about 1.5 seconds after.

  • Works over full-screen games
  • Voice trigger or hotkey, with 15, 30, 45 or 60 second replays
  • Imports MP4, MOV and MKV files your recorder saves to a watched folder (it reads the files; it doesn’t control the recorder)
  • Sessions, trimming, reordering and 9:16 MP4 export
  • macOS 15 or later. Windows isn’t supported yet.

A signed, notarized installer is not available yet, so there’s no download. Join the beta to hear when there is.

Web editor

Available now

No install. Upload clips you already have and cut them into one vertical video.

  • MP4, MOV, WebM or MKV, up to 250 MB each
  • Trim on the timeline, drag to reorder, preview the whole sequence
  • Blur-fill or crop for 9:16, exported at 720p or 1080p
  • Your files are sent to the server only when you press Export

Your screen stays yours

The Mac app is built so that nothing leaves your computer unless you send it.

Off until you turn it on
The replay buffer only runs while you’ve switched it on, with a visible indicator.
Speech stays on your Mac
“Clip that” is recognized on-device. If that isn’t available, voice capture stays off rather than using a server.
The buffer is temporary
Buffered footage is deleted when you stop recording or quit.
Exclude apps, pause any time
Keep a password manager or banking window out of every recording.
No automatic upload
Captures stay on your Mac. No account needed.

What isn’t here yet

We’d rather tell you now. Clip That doesn’t do AI highlight detection or beat sync yet. Edit styles are names only for the moment, and these aren’t available:

  • Automatic captions
  • Posting straight to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube
  • Windows
  • Accounts and cloud sync between the Mac app and the web editor
