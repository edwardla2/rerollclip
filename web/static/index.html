What Reroll does to your clips

The edit is meant to be postable as it comes out. It keeps the play the way it happened and leaves the rest to you.

Keeps the play at real speed
Fights, abilities and kills play exactly as they happened. No slow-mo, no filters.
Cuts the dead time
Walking, waiting and rotating get trimmed or sped through, so the video moves.
Saves the best for last
Your biggest play, like an ace, comes last, so the video ends on its high point.
Shows the whole screen
The full game frame in the middle with a blurred fill above and below. Your HUD and kill feed stay readable.
Skips repeats
If the same play got saved twice, it goes in once.
Leaves the music to you
Exports with game audio, so you can add a trending sound when you post. A generated beat is optional.

How it works

  1. Clip with Medal

    Nothing changes about how you play or record. Reroll reads the clips Medal saves to your PC.

  2. Make my video

    One click. Reroll picks your recent plays and builds the video on your PC.

  3. Post it

    Watch it, then export a 1080×1920 MP4 for TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Reels.

Runs on your PC

This is how the Windows app is being built. We’ll check each point on real gaming PCs before the beta ships.

Clips stay on your computer
Videos are made from the files Medal already saved. Nothing is uploaded, and you don’t need an account to edit.
It only reads files
Reroll reads your saved clips. It doesn’t connect to Medal’s app or to your game.
No subscription
No cloud and no monthly plan in the beta.
