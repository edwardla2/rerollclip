DESKTOP PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your screen.
Your choice.

The Mac companion is local-first. Capture stays off until you enable it. These safeguards describe the desktop app, not a future hosted web editing service.

◎On-device speech

Voice commands are recognized locally.

▣Opt-in capture

Recording starts only when you turn it on. You can exclude specific apps.

⌫Temporary replay buffer

The rolling buffer is deleted when recording stops.

⇥No automatic upload

Desktop captures remain on your machine. No account required.
