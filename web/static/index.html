Your gameplay clips shouldn’t sit in a folder forever.

Reroll is a Windows companion for Medal that turns saved gameplay clips into edited vertical highlights, automatically.

Made for your next highlight

  1. Keep recording with Medal

    No new recorder, no new habits. Clip the way you already do.

  2. Reroll finds the clip

    It watches the capture folder you choose and picks up each clip once Medal has finished saving it.

  3. Get a 9:16 edit

    Reroll trims to the moment and frames it vertically. You don’t build a timeline.

  4. Review and export

    Watch the edit, then export the MP4 for TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels.

Built to run on your PC

This is how the Windows app is being built. We’ll confirm each point on real gaming PCs before the beta ships.

Clips stay on your computer
Edits are made locally from the files Medal already saved. No upload, no account needed for editing.
It reads files, nothing more
Reroll reads your saved clips. It doesn’t connect to Medal’s app or your game.
Light while you play
Editing runs at low priority in the background. We’re measuring the impact on frame rate before we make any promises.
